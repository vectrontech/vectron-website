## Why Vectron for Rydberg / quantum-RF programs

Rydberg atom-based receivers promise calibration-free, SI-traceable field
measurement from HF to mmWave — but turning a vapor-cell physics demo into a
fielded instrument requires **classical RF rigor**: field uniformity, probe
characterization, EMI/EMC validation, antenna/aperture integration, and
traceable calibration against conventional standards. That bridge between
**atomic metrology** and **operational RF engineering** is exactly where
Vectron lives. We are a senior-only consultancy that extends a Rydberg
hardware team without a black-box handoff.

## Core competencies

**RF & Electromagnetics**
- Antenna and aperture design, integration, and measurement (printed, wire, aperture)
- Link budget, propagation, and sensitivity/dynamic-range analysis
- Independent RF simulation review — **HFSS, CST Microwave Studio, FEKO, Keysight ADS**
- Vapor-cell field-uniformity modeling and field-distortion mitigation analysis

**EMI/EMC & Field Metrology**
- EMC/EMI investigations and root-cause analysis of emissions/susceptibility failures
- RF E-field probe characterization and cross-validation
- SI-traceable calibration workflows: atom-based vs. antenna-based field measurement
- Test-plan development and measurement-uncertainty analysis

**AI/ML Automation (production-grade, spec-traceable)**
- ML-assisted signal detection, EIT-readout processing, and bandwidth/sensitivity optimization
- Requirements-to-verification pipelines with full traceability
- Simulation orchestration around HFSS, CST, ADS, and MATLAB
- Automated deliverable, report, and documentation generation from test/sim data
