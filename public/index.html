02About

Domenic Belgiovane, PhD

I help engineering teams solve challenging problems.

When a design hits a bottleneck, a problem needs a second set of expert eyes, or the workload outgrows your team, I step in. I take on antenna, RF, radar, and EM work as consulting, scoped projects, or an ongoing technical advisor role. I can also support business development and sales: technical proposals, customer meetings, and capability briefs.

Behind that is an Ohio State PhD and about 15 years of radar, antenna, and RF engineering at Raytheon, Northrop Grumman, and SpaceX. I'm an AMTA Senior Member, a U.S. citizen with an active security clearance, and based in Scottsdale. With Vectron, you get one point of contact from concept through test.

Vectron builds AI systems that plug into engineering workflows and do real work, not demos.

In plain terms: AI agents read your specs and documents, run your simulation tools, and draft the reports, with an engineer reviewing everything before it goes out. The work that took a week becomes a pipeline that runs while your team sleeps.

Agents

Agents that read specs and draft reports

Modern AI agents parse requirements from customer documents, trace them into verification artifacts, and turn sim outputs and test data into customer-ready reports in your house style.

Tool integration

Connected to your engineering tools

Models wired into CAD and simulation tools: batch-sweep harnesses around HFSS, CST, ADS, and Matlab that are triggered, logged, and archived, with results post-processed into deliverables.

Private deployment

On-prem for CUI and ITAR data

Workflows that meet CUI handling requirements and stay inside ITAR-compliant environments. Capable open-weight models run on your hardware or in sovereign-cloud regions, so no data crosses your security boundary.
