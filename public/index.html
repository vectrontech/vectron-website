About

Domenic Belgiovane, PhD

Owner and principal engineer

Vectron Technologies is the independent antenna and electromagnetics practice of Domenic Belgiovane, who holds a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The Ohio State University.

He brings about 13 years of aerospace and defense hardware experience at Raytheon, Northrop Grumman, SpaceX, and Microwave Vision Group (MVG), carrying antenna and RF hardware from concept and simulation through qualification test and production.

Through Vectron he provides antenna design, analysis, and test consulting, working as the single technical point of contact from scoping to delivery.

He also serves as a Technical Advisor to Black Knights Commercial & Combat Drones.

He is a U.S. citizen with an active security clearance, based in Scottsdale, Arizona.

Spacecraft antenna

CubeSat GPS reflectometry antenna

Designed a low-profile, circularly polarized antenna for a 3U CubeSat. Qualified from -40 to +140 C and up to 14 G vibration; measured peak gain within 0.5 dB of simulation.

Flight-deployed instrument
